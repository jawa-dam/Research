🕵️ Community investigation division

Welcome, Curious Citizen / Independent Explorer.

No degree, job title, or special vocabulary required. Every strong investigation needs people who ask honest questions, notice assumptions, find useful sources, and refuse to confuse confidence with evidence. Your curiosity belongs at the table.

THE PEOPLE'S CASE FILE

Your investigation assignment

Help examine the proposal by asking questions, checking sources, spotting unclear claims, offering alternative explanations, and sharing constructive criticism. You do not need to decide whether GEI is right.

Your first challenge

What part of this idea needs a better explanation, a stronger source, or a fair challenge?

What you can bring to the crew

  • Curiosity and a willingness to ask clear questions
  • Useful sources, lived practical knowledge, careful observations, or a different perspective
  • Respectful disagreement and a readiness to revise your own view when evidence changes

What you may gain

  • A way to participate without academic credentials
  • Practice evaluating claims and sources rather than simply accepting or rejecting them
  • A voice in a research discussion where thoughtful questions are welcome

How the crew works

Choose a specific question. Examine sources or assumptions. Record what supports or challenges the proposal. Share what you find with clarity and respect.

No agreement required

Your role matters

You're not here to defend a conclusion.

GEI is a proposed interpretive framework under investigation. Your contribution may support a comparison, narrow its limits, identify a better explanation, or show that an idea does not hold up. All of those outcomes are useful.
