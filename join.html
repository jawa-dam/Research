The team protocol

Not a belief test. An investigation.

GEI is a proposed interpretive framework—not an established translation, proven ancient technology, or academic consensus. The team is here to examine what holds up, what needs revision, and what does not work.

01 · BRIEF

Choose a question

Start with one claim or comparison in your area of interest.

02 · INVESTIGATE

Bring your tools

Use sources, methods, calculations, language skills, or careful questions.

03 · CHALLENGE

Find the weak spot

Record counterevidence, limits, alternative explanations, and possible falsifiers.

04 · REPORT

Share what you find

Help document what the evidence supports—and what it does not.

Our ground rule: You are never expected to defend GEI. You are invited to examine it. No one should have to pretend a weak argument is strong, or a question is settled when it is not.
Before you enter

What joining does—and does not—mean

Joining means: expressing interest in an open research discussion, choosing a question, and contributing respectfully within your experience or expertise.

Joining does not mean: endorsing GEI, accepting a religious position, giving up personal beliefs, receiving a professional credential, being guaranteed publication, employment, or compensation, or representing an institution.

Public attribution is optional and should only happen with your explicit permission. Do not submit confidential information or sensitive personal details in an initial inquiry.
